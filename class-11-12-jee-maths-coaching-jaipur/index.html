What we teach in Classes 11–12

The senior batches combine board maths with JEE preparation. Topics include sets and functions, trigonometry, sequences and series, complex numbers, permutations and combinations, coordinate geometry, limits, differentiation and integration, matrices and determinants, vectors, three-dimensional geometry and probability.

JEE maths prep

The founder, Tr. Ishaan Taneja, secured All India Rank 7041 in JEE Advanced and leads this preparation.

  • Problem-solving depth. Students move beyond standard exercises to multi-step questions that combine several concepts.
  • Previous-year questions. Regular practice with past JEE Main and JEE Advanced papers shows how each topic is examined.
  • Timed mock tests. Full-length and chapter-wise tests help students work on speed and accuracy under exam timing.
  • Doubt sessions. Dedicated time to clear questions that come up during self-study.

We do not promise ranks or selections; our aim is careful, honest preparation.

Batches and timings

Seniors study in groups of ten, offline in Raja Park or live online. Classes run 8 AM–8 PM, Monday to Saturday, one hour a day. Parents get a WhatsApp note on what each lesson covered.

Questions parents ask

Yes. Maths preparation covers both papers through previous-year questions, timed mock tests and doubt sessions, alongside the Class 11–12 board syllabus.

Can I take 1-on-1 JEE maths lessons?

Yes. Private sessions for Classes 11–12 cost ₹999 an hour, online or at the studio.
