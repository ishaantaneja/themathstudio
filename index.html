Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which grades do you teach?

The Math Studio teaches mathematics from Grade 1 to Grade 12. Grades 11 and 12 cover board mathematics along with JEE foundation.

How many students are in a batch?

Every batch is capped at 10 students, so each child gets personal attention.

What are the class timings?

Batches run Monday to Saturday in nine slots: 6–7 AM, 7–8 AM, 8–9 AM, 9–10 AM, 10–11 AM, 4–5 PM, 5–6 PM, 6–7 PM and 7–8 PM. Sundays are off. The centre is open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

Where is The Math Studio located?

2nd Floor, Near Govind Marg, Raja Park (Near Hyundai Showroom), Jaipur – 302004, Rajasthan.

When is the scholarship test?

The first scholarship test is on 5 December 2026 at the centre, and tests are held on the 5th of every month after that. The test is offline. The syllabus is the full mathematics curriculum for the grade the student selects. Registration is free.

How is the monthly fee decided?

Each grade band has a listed monthly fee: Grades 1–5 ₹4,500, Grades 6–8 ₹6,000, Grades 9–10 ₹7,500, and Grades 11–12 ₹9,500. Your scholarship test score sets the discount: 96% and above earns a full scholarship, 90–95% earns 70% off, 80–89% earns 50% off, 70–79% earns 30% off, and 60–69% earns 15% off. Below 60%, the listed monthly fee applies.

Do you offer a demo class?

Yes. You can book a free demo class before you enrol. Use the Book a demo button or the contact form to register your interest.

How does admission work?

Admission has four steps: enquire, book a free demo class, take the scholarship test, and enrol in a batch that fits your grade and preferred time slot.
