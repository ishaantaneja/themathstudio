Our mission

Math Wrath exists so that money is never the reason a child gives up on math. We're building two things: free online math classes for kids who can't afford coaching, and an affordable in-person institute in Raja Park, Jaipur, where fees are discounted for students in need.

Quality coaching

Clear concepts, steady practice, and teachers who care about understanding, not only marks.

Affordable by design

Fees are kept low, with further discounts for students who need them.
