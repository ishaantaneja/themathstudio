Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which grades do you teach?

The Math Studio teaches mathematics from Grade 1 to Grade 12. Grades 11 and 12 cover board mathematics along with JEE foundation.

How many students are in a batch?

Never more than 10, so each child gets personal attention.

What are the class timings?

We teach from 6 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday. Offline batches at Raja Park run at fixed one-hour times within those hours. For online classes you can choose any one-hour slot between 6 AM and 9 PM; please book at least 24 hours ahead, subject to availability. Sundays are off.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Every grade is available offline at our Raja Park studio and live online, with the same teaching standard, the same batch size of up to 10, and the same class summaries for parents.

How much are the fees?

Monthly plans: Grades 1–5 ₹2,499, Grades 6–8 ₹3,499, Grades 9–10 ₹3,999, Grades 11–12 ₹4,499. You can also pay per online class, with no commitment, or choose a yearly plan and save about 20%. One-on-one lessons start at ₹499 an hour.

When is the scholarship test?

The first scholarship test is on 5 December 2026 at the centre, and tests are held on the 5th of every month after that. The test is offline. The syllabus is the full mathematics curriculum for the grade the student selects. Registration is free.

How does the scholarship work?

Your score in our monthly scholarship test earns a discount on your first month's fee for offline batches only: 96% and above: 100%; 90–95%: 70%; 80–89%: 50%; 70–79%: 30%; 60–69%: 15%. Regular fees apply from the second month.

Do you have a referral offer?

Yes. You get 15% off your next month's fee for every student you refer who enrols, up to 45% in a month. Your friend gets 15% off their first month too.

Do you offer a demo class?

Yes, two free demo classes, offline at Raja Park or online, so you can judge the teaching properly before you enrol.

Will I know what my child learned in class?

Yes. Parents get a written summary on WhatsApp after every class.

Is there a registration fee?

No. There's no registration fee, no material fee, and no hidden charges.

What if we want to stop?

Pay-per-class has no commitment at all. For monthly plans, you can simply not renew. For yearly plans, unused fees are refunded on a pro-rata basis.

Where is The Math Studio located?

2nd Floor, Near Govind Marg, Raja Park (Near Hyundai Showroom), Jaipur – 302004, Rajasthan.

How does admission work?

Admission has four steps: enquire, book two free demo classes, take the scholarship test, and enrol in an offline batch of 10 or pick your own online hour between 6 AM and 9 PM.
